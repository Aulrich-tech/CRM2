Module 2: CRM Technology and Data Platforms

How technology helps businesses collect, organize, and use customer information.

CRM Technology

DefinitionHelps businesses collect, organize, access, and use customer information efficiently.

Why CRM Needs Technology

Customer Management
Streamlines data collection, ensuring employees can access vital information quickly.
Enhanced Efficiency
Automates routine tasks and organizes customer interactions.

Technology's Role in CRM

Data Collection
Allows businesses to gather essential customer information.
Information Sharing
Seamless sharing of customer insights across marketing teams.
Pattern Analysis
Analyzes customer behavior patterns.
Data Organization
Organizes customer data systematically.
Activity Automation
Routine tasks can be automated.

CRM Platform

DefinitionA centralized system that integrates customer information and interactions.

Understanding Customer Touchpoints in CRM

Multiple Interaction Channels
Customer engages with businesses through various channels.
Unified Customer Experience
Connects all customer interactions.
Enhanced Data Management
Enables businesses to gather and analyze customer data efficiently.

Major CRM Applications

Marketing Automation
Automating in CRM streamlines campaigns.
Sales Management
Helps track leads, opportunities, and forecasts.
Customer Support
Support by organizing service requests. Tracking complaints and providing staff with customer history.

The Three CRMs

Operational CRM
Generating data to streamline processes.
Analytical CRM
Customer information to understand behavior and trends.
Collaborative CRM
Facilitates information sharing across departments.

How CRM Types Collaborate

Unified Data Flow
Capture customer transactions.
Enhanced Customer Experience
Businesses can personalize interactions.

Cloud-Based CRM

DefinitionAccess customer data and CRM applications over the internet.

Advantages of Cloud CRM

  • Accessibility
  • Cost-Effectiveness
  • Scalability

  • Easy Updates
  • Collaboration

Understanding CRM Integration and Its Importance

Centralized Data Access
Allows businesses to access all customer data in one place.
Enhance Collaboration
Departments can share vital information seamlessly.
Improved Customer Experience
Provides a cohesive experience for customers by connecting touchpoints.
